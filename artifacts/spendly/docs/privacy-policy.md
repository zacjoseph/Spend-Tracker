# Privacy Policy for Multi Currency Spend

**Last updated:** August 13, 2026

Multi Currency Spend ("the app") is a personal spending tracker developed by Zachary Friedman. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

## Summary

Multi Currency Spend is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not operate user accounts, and we do not sell your personal information.

## Information the app stores

When you use Multi Currency Spend, the app stores the following **locally on your device**:

- Expense entries (amount, category, note, date, and currency)
- Your currency preferences and custom currencies
- Exchange rates cached from a third-party service

This data is saved using your phone's local storage (AsyncStorage). It is not uploaded to our servers because we do not operate backend servers for your spending data.

## Information we do not collect

Multi Currency Spend does **not** collect:

- Your name, email address, or phone number
- Location data
- Contacts or photos (unless you explicitly choose a backup file from your device)
- Analytics or advertising identifiers
- Payment or bank account information

## Third-party services

### Exchange rate data

Multi Currency Spend fetches public currency exchange rates from [open.er-api.com](https://open.er-api.com) so it can convert amounts between currencies. When rates are refreshed, your device makes a network request to that service. We do not send your expense data as part of that request.

Please review the third-party provider's terms and privacy practices separately.

## Backup and restore

Multi Currency Spend lets you export and import a backup file. Backup files are created and stored by **you** — for example, in iCloud, Google Drive, email, or another app you choose. We do not receive or store your backup files.

You are responsible for keeping your backup files secure.

## Children's privacy

Multi Currency Spend is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data retention and deletion

Your expense data remains on your device until you delete it in the app (for example, using "Clear all expenses" in Settings) or uninstall the app.

Uninstalling Multi Currency Spend removes locally stored app data from your device.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent version. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
